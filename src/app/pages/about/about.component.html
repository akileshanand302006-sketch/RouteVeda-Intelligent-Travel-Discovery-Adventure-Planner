About RouteVeda

A futuristic travel planning platform built as an advanced Angular mini project.

Forge Your Journey with Precision

RouteVeda is designed to solve the complexity of adventure travel planning. By combining destination discovery, real-time activity selection, dynamic budget calculations, and automated day-by-day itinerary generation into one seamless interface, RouteVeda provides travelers with complete control over their journeys.
